About KisanLens

KisanLens is a hackathon prototype that shows how image-classification AI can give farmers a quick first look at leaf health.

How it works

A MobileNetV2 neural network, pre-trained on general images and then trained on labelled tomato and potato leaf photos, estimates the probability of each supported class. Photos are checked and resized on the server; nothing is guessed if the model is missing.

Limits you should know

  • Only tomato and potato leaves are supported, and only the listed conditions.
  • The training photos were mostly taken against plain backgrounds. Real field photos can look very different and results may be less accurate.
  • It cannot tell nutrient shortage, herbicide damage or many other problems from disease.
  • It does not give chemical names or doses. Always follow local expert advice.

Privacy

Uploaded photos are re-encoded, stored under random names on the server, and shown only to the browser session that uploaded them. You can delete saved scans from the Scan History page.

Data credit

Model training uses the PlantVillage dataset (Hughes & Salathé, 2015). Disease information should be checked against the references listed in the Disease Library.
