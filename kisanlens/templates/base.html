KisanLens

An AI-assisted crop health project built for SETU 2026. It supports tomato and potato leaves only.

Responsible use

KisanLens gives an automated estimate from a photo. It can be wrong and does not replace an agricultural expert. Always follow locally approved recommendations before spraying or treating a crop.
