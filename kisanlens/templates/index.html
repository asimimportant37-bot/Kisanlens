Understand Your Crop

A photo is only a starting point. These tips help you get a useful result and use it wisely.

Take a useful leaf photo

Use daylight, not harsh sun or flash. Fill the frame with one leaf, keep it in focus, and show both healthy and affected parts. A plain background helps.

Similar symptoms, different causes

Fungi, bacteria, viruses, pests, nutrient shortage and weather damage can all cause spots or yellowing. The same-looking leaf may need very different care.

Why predictions can be wrong

The model learned mostly from clean, close-up photos. Blurry images, shadows, dirt, other diseases or crops it was not trained on can fool it. A high confidence score is not proof.

Ask an expert

Show the affected plant to your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), agriculture officer or trusted agronomist, especially if the problem is spreading.

Follow local recommendations

Approved products, doses and timing depend on your state, crop stage and weather. KisanLens never gives chemical doses; follow your local agriculture department's advice.
